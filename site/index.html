    Frequently Asked Questions

    Search or expand common questions about attendance, workshops, and logistics
    Is DevFest free to attend?

    Yes! DevFest is a 100% free, community-organized developer conference supported by our sponsors and volunteer community. Advance registration is required.

    Do I need to register in advance?

    Yes. Venue capacity is capped at 500 attendees. Completing the RSVP form reserves your pass and generates your conference badge immediately.

    What should I bring to hands-on workshops?

    Bring a charged laptop, charger, and a valid photo or student ID for registration check-in. High-speed Wi-Fi and power strips are provided in the Workshop room.

    How do I save my personal schedule and export it to my calendar?

    Click the star (☆) button on any session in the Schedule section to save it to "My Agenda", then click "Export .ics" to download a standard RFC 5545 calendar file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

    Will talks and slides be recorded?

    All Main Hall keynotes and technical talks are recorded and published with speaker slide decks after the conference.

    Code of Conduct

    Our commitment to a safe, welcoming, and harassment-free conference environment

    DevFest is dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive conference experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, or religion.

    We do not tolerate harassment of conference participants in any form. All attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers at DevFest are required to agree with and uphold this Code of Conduct throughout all sessions, workshops, and community networking events.
