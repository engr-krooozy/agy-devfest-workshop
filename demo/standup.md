name: standup
description: Write a three-bullet standup update from the last 10 commits
---

Read the last 10 commits and write a three-bullet standup update.

- Bullet 1: what shipped
- Bullet 2: what is in progress
- Bullet 3: what is blocked, or "nothing" if clear

Keep each bullet under 15 words. Plain language, no commit hashes.
